# Investment Banking Operations Portfolio

A learning portfolio documenting key concepts, transaction processes, and case studies in investment banking operations.

## Areas Covered

- Investment Banking Fundamentals
- Financial Markets and Capital Markets
- Mergers and Acquisitions (M&A)
- Equity Capital Markets (ECM) and IPO Process
- Bonds and Fixed Income
- Trade Life Cycle
- Reference Data Management

## Case Studies

### 1. HDFC Ltd–HDFC Bank Merger

- Strategic rationale and business fit
- Share-swap structure and regulatory approvals
- Transaction considerations, integration, and key risks
- Expected business benefits

### 2. Tata Capital IPO

- IPO structure and pricing considerations
- Book-building mechanism
- Role of investment banks in equity capital markets
- Investor considerations and listing process

## Learning Objectives

- Understand key stages of investment banking transactions.
- Develop familiarity with capital markets and financial instruments.
- Learn the importance of data accuracy, transaction processing, and operational controls.
- Document concepts and case studies for continued learning.
